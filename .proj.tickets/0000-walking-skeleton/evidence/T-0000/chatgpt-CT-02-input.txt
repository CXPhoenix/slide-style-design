# Takahashi style

Provide reusable style guidance directly. Content transformation and presentation
production belong to the consuming tools.

## Identifying core

`takahashi.text_primary` is the mandatory core rule:

- Words or phrases carry the primary visual/message focus.
- Relative scale establishes their priority.
- Other detail, if present, supports that text focus rather than becoming an equal
  or dominant focal point. Supporting charts or notes are not universally excluded.

This relationship is source-led project synthesis, not a creator quotation or
universal numerical formula. Consult the [core/source record](references/core-rule.md)
when source attribution, applicability, evidence limits, or boundary examples matter.
The core above is sufficient for a direct invocation; no route is required.

## Guidance boundary

Use supplied material as context for style constraints. Preserve any qualifications
needed to interpret the material; style guidance is not a reason to remove them.
Return guidance and constraints, not rewritten material, page plans, presentation
files, or rehearsal instructions. Consuming tools can separately perform authorized
content or production work using this guidance.

## Core/YAML handoff

This walking-skeleton edition provides core guidance in Taiwan Traditional Chinese.
Field names and rule identifiers remain English. JSON switching, adjustment response
handling, other language variants, and route behavior are separate increments.

Return complementary Markdown and one YAML settings block. Include the style

Background, source reasoning, and illustrative examples may stay Markdown-only.

The YAML contains `style_id: takahashi`, a `core_rules` list containing the mandatory

# Takahashi core/source record

## Rule identity

- Rule ID: `takahashi.text_primary`.
- Rule classification: core.
- Evidence class: project synthesis, grounded in direct publication support and
  limited observations of creator material; not a quotation or official rule ID.

## Required propositions and applicability

1. Words or phrases are the primary visual/message carrier in Takahashi guidance.
2. Relative scale establishes their priority rather than a fixed font-size rule.
3. Other detail, if included, is supporting rather than an equal or dominant focus.

Preserve the third proposition as conditional: supporting detail may be absent.
When it is present, preserve its relationship to the primary words/phrases.
Maintain source-material qualifications needed for correct interpretation. This
record describes guidance, not a procedure for rewriting or splitting slides.

## Evidence basis and reading limits

**T1 — Direct publication support.** The creator's publisher describes very large
text as a defining feature of the method. This supports relative textual emphasis;
the primary-carrier/supporting-detail relationship above is the project's practical
synthesis, not a claim that the publisher states all three propositions.
Source: [SB Creative, the creator's 2005 book](https://www.sbcr.jp/product/4797332530/).
Reading scope: publisher introduction and bibliographic material, not the full book.

**T2 — Limited creator-sample observation.** The creator's later LT material shows
short textual fragments and also includes bullets and relationship-model examples
in extracted text/alternative text. This limits the claim that all creator
presentations universally exclude other forms. It does not prove every chart fits
the original text-first method.
Source: [Takahashi's 2011 LT material](https://www.slideshare.net/takahashim/ldd11-w).
Reading scope: extracted web text/alternative text; no full visual measurement,
downloaded PDF, or observed delivery timing. Source notes were collected 2026-10-03.

No fixed font size, word count, pacing, or density threshold is established by these
reading scopes. Any later numerical project choice needs its own explicit status
and conditions. No universal audience-effectiveness claim is supported here.

## Boundary examples

**Compatible:** A chart and three auxiliary note lines support the main words/phrases;
relative scale still makes those words the main visual/message focus. Three is an
illustrative case, not an allowed-count threshold.

**Conflicting:** A dense chart or prose paragraph becomes the main message carrier,
while the words/phrases are only a minor heading. This replaces the required
primary/supporting relationship.

These examples judge the meaning of guidance. They do not assess a rendered slide
or introduce the adjustment-response handling delivered by a later ticket.

## 高橋流指引

核心規則 `takahashi.text_primary`：以詞句承載主要視覺與訊息，透過相對尺度
建立優先地位。其他細節若出現，須輔助這個文字焦點，不能成為同等或更主要
的焦點。保留理解素材所需的限定條件；這些指引交由其他工具處理實際製作。

這是根據來源整理的專案規則，不是創作者原話，也沒有固定字數或字體大小。

```yaml
style_id: takahashi
core_rules:
  - rule_id: takahashi.text_primary
    constraint: >-
      以詞句作為主要視覺與訊息載體，透過相對尺度建立優先地位；
      若包含其他細節，須讓它們輔助詞句，而非成為同等或更主要的焦點。
      保留正確理解素材所需的限定條件。
adjustable_rules: []
```
