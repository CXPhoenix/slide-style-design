# English guidance example

Steve Jobs style: jobs.single_focus. Several products support one stated battery-life comparison. Their text and visuals support that shared point, not competing claims. Requested colors are compatible; preserve common test conditions and limitations.

These rules are source-led project synthesis, not creator quotations or official numerical formulas. Style constraints only; no content transformation.

style_id: jobs
core_rules:
- rule_id: jobs.single_focus
  constraint: Display, benefit, reveal or comparison elements support one stated focal point. Text and visual elements support that focus rather than independent competing claims. Several products may jointly support one battery-life comparison; preserve the common test conditions and limited applicability.
adjustable_rules:
- rule_id: jobs.presentation_preferences
  constraint: Use the requested colors and several objects for the same battery-life comparison, without competing focal claims; retain common test conditions and applicability limits.
