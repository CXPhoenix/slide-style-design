# Steve Jobs style

Provide reusable style guidance directly. Content transformation and presentation
production belong to consuming tools.

## Identifying core

`jobs.single_focus` is the mandatory core rule:

- Display, benefit, reveal or comparison elements support one stated focal point.
- Text and visual elements support that point rather than becoming independent,
  competing primary claims. Use concise text and relevant visual evidence to make
  the relationship clear.
- Several objects can serve the same comparison. One focal point is not a rule
  that only one object may appear; color or background alone does not identify it.

This is source-led project synthesis, not Jobs's quotation or official method.
Consult the [core/source record](references/core-rule.md) for attribution,
applicability, reading limits or boundary examples. The core above is sufficient
for direct use; no route prerequisite applies.

## Guidance boundary

Use supplied material as context for style constraints. Preserve conditions and
qualifications needed to interpret a benefit or comparison, including shared test
conditions and limits on generalization when supplied. Concision cannot remove
those qualifications. Explain the expression relationship without rewriting the
material, splitting pages, inventing a deck-wide narrative or producing files.
Consuming tools may separately perform authorized content or production work.

Do not turn a keynote excerpt into a mandatory buildup/reveal/demo/recap sequence,
fixed timing, word limit or object-count formula. Reveal is one possible focal
point, not a requirement to construct a speaking itinerary.

## Adjustments

Accept requested non-core preferences that retain every `jobs.single_focus`
proposition and applicable material qualification. Color changes and several objects
serving one comparison are compatible; object count or background alone is not a
conflict. State the actual accepted preference in Markdown and settings under
`jobs.presentation_preferences`, retaining the complete core record.
Consult the [adjustment record](references/adjustments.md) for classification,
conditions and evidence limits, and the [compatible example](references/compatible-example.md)
when applying color or related objects. These are expression constraints; content
restructuring, splitting or appendix relocation remain consuming-tool decisions.

## Core conflicts

When a request negates/replaces a core proposition, name `jobs.single_focus`,
quote/paraphrase the request, explain the broken focal/supporting relationship and
offer a retaining suggestion. Deliver original core guidance and settings with
applicable qualifications. Exclude the conflicting override and any unaccepted
alternative from applied settings. Keep independently requested compatible choices,
but do not infer acceptance of a proposed alternative. No style switch or new
permission workflow is needed to deliver the core.
See the [conflict example](references/conflict-example.md) for unrelated competing
claims. Finish with the conflict explained and settings retaining all core conditions.

## Core/YAML handoff

This increment returns guidance in Taiwan Traditional Chinese, with English field
names and rule IDs. Other language variants, JSON switching and
routing are separate increments.

Return complementary Markdown and exactly one YAML settings block. Identify
Steve Jobs style and `jobs.single_focus` in the explanation. Both Markdown and
its constraint retain the focal-point/supporting-elements relationship and all
applicable conditions. Source reasoning and illustrative examples may be Markdown-only.

Use `style_id: jobs`, `core_rules` containing the mandatory record, and
an `adjustable_rules` list of accepted applicable preferences, or `[]` when none. Records use unique nonempty English `rule_id` values and
nonempty string `constraint` values; YAML types are JSON-compatible, without
custom tags. Finish when explanation and settings agree on the relationship and
conditions. Consult the [handoff example](references/handoff-example.md) for output
shape; wording may vary without changing identity or meaning.

If requested supporting material is inaccessible, disclose that limit rather than
invent its contents. No shell, local filesystem or connected MCP service is assumed.

# Jobs adjustment rule record

ID: `jobs.presentation_preferences`. Classification: adjustable. Evidence class:
project synthesis / user-selected preference, not creator quotation or official menu.
Basis: accepted non-core/core boundary and the [core/source record](core-rule.md).
J1 illustrates several related objects; J3 informs benefit focus. Neither establishes
mandatory colors, counts or a universal layout. Reuse their edited-excerpt/press-release
reading limits; no full keynote or rendered-slide measurement added.

Apply only to requested compatible appearance or supporting-element preferences.
State accepted changes and applicable conditions in both Markdown and constraint.
Display, benefit, reveal or comparison elements support one stated focal point;
text/visual elements support it rather than independent competing primary claims.
Preserve necessary interpretation qualifications. With no applicable preference,
this optional record is absent; no default color or object count is invented.

Compatible example: a colored background and four products serve one battery-life
comparison with test-specific limits retained. Four is case-specific, not a threshold.
Conflicting example: unrelated battery, recruitment and lunch claims receive equal
primary status without a shared point. Color cannot repair that lost relationship.
These judgments concern style guidance, not rewriting or producing a slide.

# Compatible Jobs adjustment example

## 賈伯斯流相容調整

`jobs.single_focus`：展示、效益、揭露或比較元素支援一個明確焦點；文字與視覺共同
服務焦點，不形成互相競爭的主要主張。本例四款產品共同支援同一測試條件下的
電池續航比較，保留「結果不代表其他使用情境」。

接受 `jobs.presentation_preferences`：彩色背景與 A、B、C、D 四款產品仍服務這個
比較；不是四個獨立主要主張。這是專案綜整的偏好選項，非創作者固定顏色或數量公式。
只提供風格約束，內容改寫與製作由其他工具處理。

```yaml
style_id: jobs
core_rules:
  - rule_id: jobs.single_focus
    constraint: >-
      展示、效益、揭露或比較元素支援一個明確陳述的焦點；文字與視覺服務焦點，
      不成為互相競爭的主要主張。多個物件可共同服務一項比較。保留本例共同測試條件，
      且結果不代表其他使用情境。
adjustable_rules:
  - rule_id: jobs.presentation_preferences
    constraint: >-
      採用彩色背景，同時呈現 A、B、C、D 四款產品，共同服務同一測試條件下的
      電池續航比較；維持單一比較焦點，保留結果不代表其他使用情境的限定。
```

# Jobs core conflict example

## 賈伯斯流核心衝突

要求「電池續航、企業招聘、午餐選單三個互不相關的主張皆為同等主要焦點」，

保留核心的建議是採用一個明確展示／效益／揭露／比較焦點，讓其他元素支援它；
此建議尚未接受，不列入已套用偏好，也不改寫或重組素材。

交付原本核心：展示、效益、揭露或比較元素支援一個明確焦點，文字與視覺支援
該焦點而不互相競爭；多個物件可支援同一比較，必要限定仍保留。
此關係是來源啟發的專案綜整，不是 Jobs 的官方方法或固定物件數公式。

style_id: jobs
core_rules:
  - rule_id: jobs.single_focus
    constraint: >-
      展示、效益、揭露或比較元素支援一個明確陳述的焦點；文字與視覺服務焦點，
      不成為互相競爭的主要主張。多個物件可共同服務一項比較，保留正確理解素材
      所需的條件與限定。
adjustable_rules: []
```

# Jobs core/source record

## Rule identity

- Rule ID: `jobs.single_focus`.
- Classification: core.
- Evidence class: project synthesis grounded in recorded keynote observations and
  official benefit expression; not an official rule name or creator quotation.

## Required propositions and applicability

1. Display, benefit, reveal or comparison elements support one stated focal point.
2. Text/visual elements support that point, rather than independent competing claims.

Concise text and relevant visual evidence clarify this relationship. Several objects
can serve one comparison; count alone does not determine compliance. The alternatives
(display, benefit, reveal, comparison) need not all occur at once. A generic theme,
black background or product photo without a focal relationship is insufficient.
Preserve supplied conditions and qualifications needed to interpret the focal
point; for a comparison these can include shared test conditions and limits on
applicability. These are guidance constraints, not content transformation steps.

## Evidence basis and reading limits

**J1 — Recorded keynote observation.** The Archive's 2007 Macworld excerpt describes
an introduction culminating in one device and a comparison involving multiple
phones. This motivates focus supported by related elements; it is not a universal
one-object prohibition or proof of a whole-deck formula.
Source: [Steve Jobs Archive, Make Something Wonderful](https://book.stevejobsarchive.com/).
Reading scope: the Macworld 2007 excerpt and bracketed screen/stage descriptions,
rechecked as web text on 2026-10-03. The source states it is edited/excerpted.
No complete keynote playback or slide-by-slide visual/timing measurement was done.

**J3 — Official benefit expression.** Apple's iPod launch release links capacity to
portable listening utility. This informs understandable benefit focus; a release
is not evidence of a slide layout or proof that all numbers must avoid tables.
Source: [Apple, Apple Presents iPod (2001)](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2001/10/23Apple-Presents-iPod/).
Reading scope: launch headline, product/benefit text and Jobs quotation, rechecked
2026-10-03. No layout inference is treated as direct source evidence.

The complete supporting-elements relationship is the project's practical synthesis,
not a claim that either source states this core rule verbatim. No fixed word count,
font size, object count, timing, image ratio, universal chart/table ban or mandatory
buildup/reveal/demo/recap sequence is established. No general effectiveness claim
across audiences is made.

## Boundary examples

**Compatible:** Four products and a concise comparison label support one stated
comparison point. Their colors can vary while every element retains that relationship;
necessary test-condition notes remain readable supporting information. Four is an
illustrative example, not a threshold.

**Conflicting:** Several unrelated claims receive equal primary status, with no
shared display, benefit, reveal or comparison focus. A black background or larger
product photo does not repair the missing relationship.

These examples judge guidance statements, not rendered slides or a new
content transformation. See [adjustment record](adjustments.md) for requested preferences.

精簡文字與相關視覺證據支持這個焦點，而不是各自形成競爭的主要主張。
多個物件可以服務同一比較。保留正確理解焦點所需的條件與限定，不能為了
精簡而省略。這是來源啟發的專案綜整，不是 Jobs 的原話、固定物件數或全局敘事公式。
內容改寫與簡報製作由其他工具處理。

style_id: jobs

      讓展示、效益、揭露或比較元素支持一個明確陳述的焦點；
      文字與視覺元素須支持該焦點，而非形成各自競爭的主要主張。
      多個物件可服務同一比較；保留正確理解焦點所需的條件與限定。

```
