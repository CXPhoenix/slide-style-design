# Wangxing style — 張忘形

Provide reusable style guidance directly. Content transformation and presentation
production belong to consuming tools.

## Identity and identifying core

This style refers to 張忘形, not 王興. `wangxing` is this project's identifier,
not a claim about the creator's official English name.

`wangxing.text_visual_relation` is the mandatory core rule:

- Concise viewpoint text and a visual representation communicate the same viewpoint
  or an explicitly named relationship.
- The visual contributes to understanding that point rather than serving only as
  decoration. Explain the correspondence, using supplied audience context when
  available to keep it understandable and relevant.

Color, absence of humor and independent reading can retain this core. Monochrome,
memes and oral-only use are not requirements. Appearance alone cannot substitute
for the viewpoint-to-visual relationship.

The fixed rule ID and operational relationship are source-led project synthesis,
not a creator quotation. Consult the [core/source record](references/core-rule.md)
for attribution, conditions, reading limits or boundary examples. The core above
is sufficient for direct use; no route prerequisite applies.

## Guidance boundary

Use existing material as context for style constraints. Preserve conditions and
qualifications needed to understand the viewpoint or named relationship. For
independent reading, guidance must keep the meaning and qualifications accessible
without assuming a speaker will supply them. For projection, readable supporting
information still preserves necessary conditions; concision cannot erase them.

Return style guidance rather than rewritten viewpoints, new illustrations, page
plans, whole-deck stories or presentation files. Explain how an existing visual
relates to a viewpoint without generating replacement content. Consuming tools can
separately perform authorized content or production work using the guidance.
Do not attribute fixed word/image counts, meme frequency, timing or density metrics
to the creator without supporting evidence.

## Core/YAML handoff

This increment returns Taiwan Traditional Chinese guidance with English fields
and rule IDs. Other language variants, JSON switching, adjustments and route
behavior are separate increments.

Return complementary Markdown and exactly one YAML settings block. Identify 張忘形
and `wangxing.text_visual_relation` in the explanation. Both Markdown and constraint
retain the text/visual correspondence, its contribution to understanding and all
applicable conditions. Source reasoning and illustrative examples may be Markdown-only.

Use `style_id: wangxing`, `core_rules` containing the mandatory record, and
`adjustable_rules: []`. Records use unique nonempty English `rule_id` values and
nonempty string `constraint` values, with JSON-compatible YAML types and no custom
tags. Finish when guidance and settings agree on meaning and conditions. Consult
the [handoff example](references/handoff-example.md) for output shape; wording may
vary while identity and meaning remain intact.

If requested supporting material is inaccessible, disclose that limit rather than

# Wangxing core/source record

## Identity and classification

- Creator identity: 張忘形, not 王興.
- Style ID: `wangxing`, a project identifier, not an official English name claim.
- Mandatory rule ID: `wangxing.text_visual_relation`.
- Classification: core.
- Evidence class: source-led project synthesis grounded in creator descriptions;
  the rule ID and complete operational propositions are not a creator quotation.

## Required propositions and applicability

1. Concise viewpoint text and a visual representation communicate the same viewpoint
   or an explicitly named relationship.
2. The visual contributes to understanding that point, rather than decoration alone.

Name the correspondence so that it can be checked; use supplied audience context
when available. A picture with an unrelated caption does not satisfy the core.
Color and absence of humor may retain it; monochrome and memes are not mandatory.
The style is not restricted to spoken projection. For independently read material,
keep the viewpoint, visual relationship and needed qualifications understandable
without assuming an accompanying speaker. Projection likewise cannot remove
necessary conditions merely to simplify. Do not generalize a qualified viewpoint
beyond supplied circumstances. This is guidance, not a content-editing procedure.

## Evidence basis and reading limits

**W1 — Creator self-description.** The creator's site describes a social-media origin
and early black-and-white graphic/text expression. This supports a medium beyond
oral presentation and identifies an early appearance; it does not establish that
all uses must be monochrome or prove independent-reading effectiveness.
Source: [張忘形, 關於忘形](https://chwanshing.page/about/detail/2).
Reading scope: public self-description text, rechecked 2026-10-04. Audience/reach and
credential self-reports are not treated as independently verified outcome evidence.

**W2 — Creator description of the approach.** The product page links concise verbal
expression with a picture to convey a viewpoint. The requirement that their
relationship contribute to understanding is the project's operational synthesis.
Source: [張忘形, 忘形流簡報思考術](https://chwanshing.page/product/%E5%BF%98%E5%BD%A2%E6%B5%81%E7%B0%A1%E5%A0%B1%E6%9B%B8%E7%B1%8D).
Reading scope: public product-description text, rechecked 2026-10-04; no complete

The evidence does not establish mandatory humor, a meme frequency, timing, fixed

**Compatible:** A colored diagram maps task blocks to team members and supports a
concise viewpoint about division of work. No joke is needed. The team-specific
qualification remains readable, including when the material is read independently.

**Conflicting:** An unrelated decorative picture is attached to a caption without
helping readers understand the viewpoint or a named relationship. Black-and-white
appearance does not repair that missing contribution.

These examples judge guidance meaning, not rendered artwork or the adjustment

# Wangxing Markdown/YAML handoff example

## 忘形流核心指引

本風格指張忘形，`wangxing` 是專案識別字。
`wangxing.text_visual_relation` 要求精簡觀點文字與視覺表現傳達同一觀點，或明確
指出兩者的關係；圖像須有助理解該觀點，不能只有裝飾作用。保留正確理解所需
的條件與限定。彩色、無幽默或自行閱讀均可保留此核心；自行閱讀時，不假設
講者補充缺少的意思或限定。這是來源啟發的專案綜整，不是固定黑白或字圖數量公式。
內容改寫與圖像製作由其他工具處理。

```yaml
style_id: wangxing
core_rules:
  - rule_id: wangxing.text_visual_relation
    constraint: >-
      讓精簡觀點文字與視覺表現傳達同一觀點，或明確指出兩者的關係；
      圖像須有助理解該觀點，而非只有裝飾作用。
      保留正確理解所需的條件與限定；彩色、無幽默與自行閱讀不妨礙此核心。
      自行閱讀時，須讓觀點、圖文關係與必要限定能在沒有講者補充的情況下理解。
adjustable_rules: []
```
