# Community Code of Conduct

[繁體中文（台灣）](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) · [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.en.md)

People of different backgrounds, languages, experience and abilities should be able to discuss presentation styles and agent design safely. This policy applies to project issues, PRs, discussions and activities where someone represents this project.

## Participation

- Be respectful and discuss ideas and evidence. Disagreement does not justify personal attacks.
- Distinguish facts, sources, observations and interpretations. Correct mistakes and help newcomers understand context.
- Respect names, forms of address, cultural differences and accessibility needs. Do not discriminate based on identity, ability, experience or tools used.
- Respect creators and licensing. Discuss a style's suitability without disparaging its creator or presenting personal preferences as universal facts.
- Protect privacy when sharing AI trials. Include only necessary, sanitized material.

Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual harassment, personal attacks, persistent disruption, disclosure of others' private information and retaliation against reporters or those assisting an investigation are unacceptable. Humor or tool-generated text does not excuse these behaviors.

## Reporting

Report privately to the project maintainer by email: **[0826@fhsh.tp.edu.tw](mailto:0826@fhsh.tp.edu.tw)**. Do not post incident details, people's names or private evidence in public issues, PRs or discussions.

A report may include where and when the incident occurred, relevant behavior, necessary evidence and the requested response. Avoid collecting or forwarding unnecessary personal information. If a maintainer is involved, contact another uninvolved maintainer through their published channel. If no suitable independent handler is available, GitHub's platform abuse-reporting mechanism is an alternative.

## Response and accountability

Maintainers should limit disclosure and evidence access to what is necessary, avoid exposing reporters' identities and consider their safety needs. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. Where disclosure to a platform or legal authority is necessary, reporters should be informed when feasible. Anyone with a conflict of interest should recuse themselves.

Depending on severity, impact and persistence, maintainers may request correction or removal, issue a reminder or formal warning, restrict participation temporarily, or impose a permanent ban. Serious threats, harassment or privacy disclosures may require immediate protective action without sequential warnings. Those involved should have an opportunity to explain where safe and feasible, and decisions and their scope should be communicated clearly.

New facts or requests for reconsideration may be submitted through the same private channel; an uninvolved maintainer should review them when one is available. Maintainers are also bound by this policy. This is an original project policy under the project's MIT License, not an official translation of a third-party code of conduct.
